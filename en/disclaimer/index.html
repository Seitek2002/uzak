Take the test

Not a diagnosis

The test helps you see what needs attention. Make health decisions together with your doctor.

What the forecast is

It is the average age reached by people who give the same answers. It does not predict your fate: within any group, some people live much longer than average and others less.

What the test is not

  • A medical examination or report.
  • A reason to start or stop treatment.
  • An assessment for insurance, work or court.

When to get help right away

Chest pain or pressure, sudden weakness in an arm or leg, trouble speaking or severe shortness of breath: call your local emergency number (112, 911, 103 or another number used in your country) without waiting for the test results.

Where the numbers come from

From published meta-analyses and cohort studies. The method page explains how they are combined.
