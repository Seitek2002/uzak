Cardiovascular disease

A past heart attack or stroke about doubles the risk, and treatment lowers it.

What we ask

Do you have heart or blood vessel disease? This question covers heart attack, stroke, coronary heart disease, heart failure and similar diagnoses.

What research found

  • Data on almost 1.2 million people: a past heart attack or stroke about doubles the risk of death.
  • Heart disease combined with diabetes raises risk more than either one alone.

How the model counts it

Years for an average 40-year-old in the world. Zero is the average answer among their peers.

Answer and its effect on the forecast
AnswerYears
No+4 months
Heart attack, stroke or heart failure−5.3 years
Other heart or blood vessel disease−2.3 years

This factor can’t be changed, so the model does not include it in your potential.

What you can do

  • Take prescribed medications every day, even when you feel well.
  • Ask your doctor about cardiac rehabilitation: supervised exercise lowers the risk of another event.

Sources

  • The Emerging Risk Factors Collaboration. Association of cardiometabolic multimorbidity with mortality (opens in a new tab). JAMA. 2015;314(1):52-60.
