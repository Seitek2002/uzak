Body mass index

Weight in kilograms divided by height in meters squared. Best from 20 to 25.

What we ask

Your height and weight The index does not tell muscle from fat. It overestimates risk in athletes with a lot of muscle, so check your waist circumference too.

What research found

  • A meta-analysis of 239 studies and 10.6 million people: the risk is lowest at a BMI of 20 to 25.
  • Every 5 units above 25 raise the risk by about a third, and a BMI of 40 or higher almost triples it.
  • Being underweight is dangerous too: with a BMI below 18.5, the risk is one and a half times as high.

How the model counts it

Years for an average 40-year-old in the world. Zero is the average answer among their peers.

What you can do

  • A six-month goal: lose 5% of your weight. That already lowers blood pressure and blood sugar.
  • Eat more protein and vegetables, fewer sweets and baked goods.
  • Weigh yourself once a week at the same time.

Sources

  • The Global BMI Mortality Collaboration. Body-mass index and all-cause mortality: individual-participant-data meta-analysis of 239 prospective studies in four continents (opens in a new tab). Lancet. 2016;388:776-786.
