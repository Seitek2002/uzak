Where you lived

Years in a country have already affected your health through health care, air, roads and everyday food.

What we ask

Where have you lived for the past 10 years? The model uses the WHO life table for this country. At the start of the forecast it weighs as much as the country where you will live, then its weight decreases.

What research found

  • In the 2023 WHO tables, life expectancy differs by more than 30 years between countries.
  • Risk does not change right after a move: health and habits built up in the old place still have an effect.

What you can do

  • The past can’t be changed, but habits left over from the old place can.
  • If you moved recently, find a doctor in your new country and have a preventive check-up.
