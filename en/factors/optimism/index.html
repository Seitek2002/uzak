Outlook

Optimists live longer, and this can partly be learned.

What we ask

What do you usually expect from the future? This question assesses the habit of expecting a good or bad outcome, which is how studies measure optimism.

What research found

  • A meta-analysis of 15 studies and 230,000 people: optimists have a 14% lower risk of death.
  • In a study following 70,000 women and 1,400 men, the most optimistic lived 11% to 15% longer and more often reached age 85.

How the model counts it

Years for an average 40-year-old in the world. Zero is the average answer among their peers.

What you can do

  • In the evening, write down three things that went well.
  • Before a difficult task, describe to yourself the best realistic outcome.
