Parents’ age

A parent’s long life points to both heredity and family habits.

What we ask

How old are your parents, or how long did they live? This question counts only deaths from disease. Accidents say nothing about heredity.

What research found

  • Data on 186,000 UK Biobank participants: each 10 years a parent lived beyond 70 lowered the children’s risk of dying from heart disease by 20%.
  • Part of the association is explained by genes, part by habits shared with parents.

How the model counts it

Years for an average 40-year-old in the world. Zero is the average answer among their peers.

What you can do

  • If your parents died early, find out the cause: it will suggest what to check first.
  • Heredity is not destiny: habits affect years of life more.

Sources

  • Atkins JL, Pilling LC, Ble A, et al. Longer-Lived Parents and Cardiovascular Outcomes: 8-Year Follow-Up in 186,000 U.K. Biobank Participants (opens in a new tab). J Am Coll Cardiol. 2016;68(8):874-875.
  • Pilling LC, Atkins JL, Bowman K, et al. Human longevity is influenced by many genetic variants: evidence from 75,000 UK Biobank participants (opens in a new tab). Aging (Albany NY). 2016;8(3):547-560.
