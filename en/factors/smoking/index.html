Smoking

One of the strongest factors you can change. Quitting extends life even at 60.

What we ask

Do you smoke? This question assesses current and past smoking. Former smokers get a lower risk than people who smoke, and it keeps falling with each year without cigarettes.

What research found

  • Among 200,000 adults in the US, smokers had about three times the mortality of people who never smoked, and their lives were more than 10 years shorter.
  • Quitting before 40 removes about 90% of the excess risk, and quitting before 55 a substantial part of it.
  • A 50-year follow-up of British doctors showed the same gap: about 10 years of life.
  • Even fewer than 10 cigarettes a day nearly double the risk of death, and smokeless tobacco raises it by 22%.

How the model counts it

Years for an average 40-year-old in the world. Zero is the average answer among their peers.

What you can do

  • Set a quit date within the next two weeks and tell the people close to you.
  • Discuss nicotine replacement therapy or medications with your doctor: they improve your chances of quitting.
  • Remove cigarettes, lighters and ashtrays from your home, and for the first weeks avoid places where you used to smoke.
