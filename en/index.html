Before you trust the forecast

Is this a diagnosis?

No. The test estimates risk from the average data of large studies. Only a doctor can make a diagnosis.

How accurate is the forecast?

The model knows average risks, not your medical history. For one person it can be off by years, so look at the factors rather than a single number.

Where do my answers go?

Nowhere. They are stored in the browser on this device, and you can delete them with one button in the report. The only thing sent out is a country lookup by IP address, without your answers.

What if I don’t have blood test results?

You can skip those four questions: the model will use average values, and the report will suggest which tests to take.

How often should I take the test?

Every three to six months. The report will show how your forecast has changed since last time.
