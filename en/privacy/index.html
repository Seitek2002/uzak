Take the test

Your answers stay with you

The test runs entirely in your browser. We see neither your answers nor your results.

What is stored

Name, sex, age, countries, height, weight, answers, the dates you took the test, the style you chose, the styles you have finished and whether sound is on. All of this is kept in the browser’s local storage (localStorage) on this device under the key uzak-v1.

It also holds your answers about pets (key uzak-pet-v1), your quests and ticked days (key uzak-game-v1), and the site language you chose (key uzak-lang).

What is sent to the server

None of your answers. The server only delivers the site’s pages, as when you open any other site. There is no sign-up, analytics or ad tracking, and no cookies are used.

Country by IP address

At the country question, your browser asks the api.country.is service once which country you are in. If it does not respond, the browser asks get.geojs.io. The service sees your IP address, as any site does, but receives no answers, name or page address. You can change the country in the list. If you return to a question you already answered, no request is made.

Picture with your result

The picture for social media is drawn in your browser and is not sent anywhere until you share it yourself. You can remove the name, forecast age and list of changes from it, as they may reveal health information.

How to delete

Use the “Delete my answers” button at the bottom of your report, the “Delete pet answers” button in the pet report, or clear the site’s data in your browser settings.

Shared computer

If other people use this device, they will see your report. Take the test in private (incognito) mode: your answers will then disappear when you close the window.
